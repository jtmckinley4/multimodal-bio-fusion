## Evidence and unresolved questions

- **Published reference tables:** The [Stage 3 benchmark comparison](../Code/stage3/01_blf_datasets.ipynb#The-five-benchmarks-BioLangFusion-reports) contains transcribed paper values, not reproduced model results. Keep score precision and metric units explicit; distinguish registry membership from verified file availability. That section identifies the separate paper sources for scores, counts, and Ab1, and labels the retained output that predates its source correction.
- **Dataset version:** Does the supplied stability CSV match BioLangFusion's version and filtering? The [dataset overview](../README.md#data) records the differing counts and overlap between train and test; inspect the [notebook audit](../Code/stage1/Stage1_stability.ipynb#Dataset-audit-for-published-comparisons) before a comparison.
- **Modality definition:** When DNA, RNA, and protein inputs derive from one coding sequence, what does an encoder difference measure? The overview's [input settings](stage1-overview.md#models-and-input-settings) distinguish that construction from GTEx's distinct inputs.
- **Dataset meaning:** What does `Value` in [mRNA_Stability.csv](../datasets/mRNA_Stability.csv) represent, how was it measured, and which sequence regions are included? The CSV header alone does not establish assay or label meaning.
- **Evaluation:** Which rows are retained, how are training and test examples selected, and what generalization claim does the split support? Check the producing code and settings rather than inferring the evaluation from a dataset column.
- **Result provenance:** If prose, outputs, reviews, or slides disagree, identify their producing versions and retain the disagreement until evidence resolves it.
- **Step 3 evidence:** Its retained October 17 fungal outputs and nine JSON records predate the October 19 initialization, checkpoint, and identity repairs. They are historical results, not a rerun of the revised implementation. No equation-setting or wide-head result was added by that revision. Reference-table agreement, shape checks, and predictive fusion gains do not establish faithful reproduction or biological alignment. The notebook retains the earlier audit details and qualifies its convolution and gate-weight hypotheses.
- **Step 3 execution identity:** New runs seed before construction and require matching configuration, model settings, ordered rows, cache bytes, source hashes, and recorded environment for reuse. Checkpoints retain scheduler and RNG state. Old result JSONs remain readable but cannot silently satisfy a new run; new outputs use a separate directory. Legacy caches require explicit unverified-input acceptance. New extraction manifests guard input order and local extraction code, but unresolved encoder revisions and extraction environment still limit producer provenance. Hashing current bytes does not repair missing historical evidence.
- **Meaning of alignment:** Distinguish biological sequence correspondence, representation similarity, and predictive complementarity. Use the [Stage 2 definition](../README.md#stage-2-defining-alignment) and the overview's [composition-control findings](stage1-overview.md#summary); compare contributor interpretations with their [source papers](../Papers/README.md).
- **Further experiments:** What comparison would distinguish the proposed explanation from alternatives? Follow the [agent planning instructions](../AGENTS.md#research-planning) and [Gantt reading route](README.md#follow-the-research-plan) to identify the relevant task and prerequisites. Use the [architecture priorities](../README.md#architecture-priorities) and [Stage 3 description](../README.md#next-steps-stage-3-fusion-experiments) for technical context. Keep literature ideas, meeting preferences, and implemented methods distinct.
