- **Stability and degradation targets:** The [stability trace](methods/data-and-targets.md#mrna-stability-standardized-decay-profiles) matches all 65,356 local observations to iCodon's experiment-profile-specific standardized decay scores; higher means relatively more stable, not hours of half-life. Repeated sequences can carry different experimental contexts. The [CoV-Vac trace](methods/data-and-targets.md#cov-vac-chemical-degradation-of-designed-rna) verifies standardized means of 68-position chemical-degradation measurements, with higher meaning more degradation. Its 81-nt inputs are crops of designed 107-nt RNAs, not verified CDS. The direction and biological scope differ despite the shared `Value` column.
- **Fungal and E. coli targets:** The [benchmark target guide](methods/data-and-targets.md#fungal-and-e-coli-benchmark-targets) verifies all fungal labels as natural logs of matched author-supplied `Exprs` values and all E. coli labels as gel-score groups 0 = low, 1 = medium and 2 = high. Earlier fungal units, normalization and organism metadata remain unresolved. These findings do not establish species or family independence; the existing helper's “published preprocessing” wording is still stronger than the evidence for its 999-nt filter. Target clarification does not change code, labels or completed comparisons.
- **GTEx and full-transcript targets:** The [GTEx guide](methods/data-and-targets.md#gtex-tissue-expression-labels) distinguishes released `log(x + 1)` tissue-averaged TPM from the additional normalization statistics our loader does not apply; the log base remains unverified. The [full-transcript guide](methods/data-and-targets.md#full-transcript-targets-half-life-and-translation) distinguishes consensus stability scores, RNA-abundance-adjusted ribosome-occupancy residuals and mean ribosome load. Neither a consensus score nor its exponential gives hours of half-life, and occupancy does not directly measure protein-production speed. Keep the exact target and evaluation metric beside biological interpretations.
