The wrapper also supplies no U-to-T conversion or uppercasing, so alphabet verification belongs before interpreting those counts. The paper describes retaining the RNA 3′ end to preserve untranslated-region context relevant to abundance; Table 1 lists 12,282 bases and Table 6 lists 12,288. Protein Table 1 lists 2,047 residues, Section 4.3 discusses 2,048 without extra processing, and Table 6 lists 1,200. These discrepancies do not establish an original crop or exclusion rule. [Paper, Section 4.3 and Tables 1, 6–7](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/8f6b3692297e49e5d5c91ba00281379c-Paper-Conference.pdf).

The configuration's `num_tokens_per_seq_nuctf_rna=2048` and `num_protein_tokens_per_seq=2048` are not consulted by the tokenizer or model forward to enforce lengths. The wrapper selects rotary positions, and the inspected NT/ESM implementations build their rotary tables from actual input length; fixed absolute-position lookups are inactive. This does not mean arbitrarily long inputs are scientifically appropriate or fit in memory. [Configuration and wrapper](https://huggingface.co/InstaDeepAI/isoformer/tree/afac2a386c8f2241faf8fad3c5971a9307c13775) · [NT implementation](https://huggingface.co/InstaDeepAI/isoformer/blob/afac2a386c8f2241faf8fad3c5971a9307c13775/modeling_esm.py) · [ESM implementation](https://huggingface.co/InstaDeepAI/isoformer/blob/afac2a386c8f2241faf8fad3c5971a9307c13775/modeling_esm_original.py).
